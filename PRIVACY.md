# VocabsCapy privacy policy

VocabsCapy is a Finnish vocabulary app. It works entirely on your device.

- **No account.** The app has no sign-up and no login.
- **No data collection.** Your words, reviews, settings and progress are stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.**
- **Network use.** The app contacts the internet only to check for app updates (a small version file and update bundles served by Expo and GitHub). No personal data is included in those requests.
- **Notifications.** Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally on your phone.
- **Backups.** If you export a backup, it is a file you choose where to save or share. We never receive it.
